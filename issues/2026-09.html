Pick of the month

Severity and management of hypercortisolism in patients with adrenocortical carcinoma and overt Cushing´s syndrome

Araujo-Castro M et al. · J Clin Endocrinol Metab · ACC Practice-informing

International retrospective cohort of 101 patients with ACC and overt Cushing's syndrome (54% metastatic). Tumour size (OR 1.02, 95% CI 1.01–1.03) and urinary free cortisol (OR 1.62, 1.03–2.57) independently predicted metastatic disease. Recurrence/progression rose with cortisol excess: 21% (UFC <2×ULN), 52% (2–5×ULN), 57% (>5×ULN). Median OS 19.4 months. Limits: retrospective, heterogeneous treatment of hypercortisolism.

Take-homeThe degree of cortisol excess behaves as a marker of aggressive disease and argues for prompt, effective cortisol control.

Succinate dehydrogenase (SDHB, SDHC, SDHD) gene variants significantly modify clinical outcomes in paraganglioma-pheochromocytoma but have a limited impact on head and neck paraganglioma

Bayley JP et al. · Genet Med · PPGL Practice-informing

International genotype–phenotype study of 2,706 patients with SDHB, SDHC or SDHD variants from 57 centres. Variant type (missense vs protein-truncating) strongly shaped outcomes in thoracic-abdominal PPGL, including metastatic risk, but had little effect on head-and-neck PGL. SDHD truncating variants were enriched among patients with combined PPGL and HNPGL; SDHB carriers developed PPGL earlier than HNPGL, and paediatric HNPGL carried a higher metastatic and multifocal risk. Limits: retrospective, heterogeneous centres; abstract gives no effect sizes.

Take-homeRisk counselling and surveillance in SDHx carriers should consider the variant type as well as the gene.

Adrenocortical carcinoma

Clinical and systemic therapies

Efficacy and safety of the CAPTEM regimen in patients with advanced adrenocortical carcinoma

Ziółkowska B et al. · Endocr Connect Full text read Hypothesis-generating

Single-centre retrospective series of 15 patients with metastatic ACC receiving capecitabine + temozolomide (first line in 3, second line or later in 12; concomitant mitotane in 12). Disease control 26.6% (all stable disease, no objective responses); median PFS 3 months (95% CI 2–6) and OS 8 months (6–13). Adverse events in 86.7%, grade 3 in one patient. Limits: very small, no comparator.

In Italy: capecitabine and temozolomide are available; their use in ACC is off-label.

Take-homeActivity is low. CAPTEM is a last-resort option for selected patients, not a new standard.

Surgery and loco-regional therapies

Adrenalectomy for adrenocortical carcinoma: A national assessment of trends in the utilization of the robotic approach

Patel DJ et al. · Surgery Practice-informing

National Cancer Database analysis of 2,481 ACC adrenalectomies (2010–2022): 65.2% open, 22.4% laparoscopic, 11.9% robotic, with robotic use rising from 4.5% to 20.7%. Margin positivity did not differ (robotic vs open OR 0.79, 95% CI 0.50–1.23), and approach was not associated with survival (robotic vs open HR 1.13, 0.82–1.55); 5-year OS 48%, 51% and 55% for open, laparoscopic and robotic. Limits: registry data, strong selection of smaller tumours for minimally invasive surgery, no recurrence or capsule-rupture data.

Take-homeReassuring for selected small, localised ACC, but does not overturn the recommendation for open surgery when invasion is suspected.

Radiology, pathology and diagnosis

18F-CETO in adrenocortical carcinoma: first clinical-translational study of an adrenocortical-targeting PET tracer

Edholm A et al. · Endocr Relat Cancer Full text read Hypothesis-generating

Bicentric retrospective study. In 20 ACC patients with matched 18F-CETO and FDG PET/CT (235 lesions), 69% of lesions were positive on both tracers, 9% on CETO only and 22% on FDG only. At initial staging CETO found more lesions than FDG in 5/13 patients; at re-staging it found fewer in 5/7 (P=0.002), and all five had ongoing or prior mitotane. In an extended cohort of 90 patients (CETO or 11C-metomidate), three uptake phenotypes showed exploratory survival differences; uptake tracked CYP11B1 expression. Limits: heterogeneous cohort, variable timing of imaging relative to treatment, no reference standard for sensitivity/specificity.

In Italy: 18F-CETO is an investigational tracer, available only within studies.

Take-home18F-CETO may add value at initial staging. After mitotane, FDG remains the more reliable tracer.

Environmental pollution is associated with poorer survival in patients with adrenocortical carcinoma: findings from a national cohort study in Italy

Puglisi S et al. · Eur J Endocrinol Hypothesis-generating

Retrospective cohort of 336 adults with ACC from 12 Italian referral centres (1990–2018). In localised disease (n=250), the 26 patients living in officially contaminated sites had shorter median RFS (13 vs 43 months) and OS (67 vs 142 months); on multivariable analysis contaminated-site residence was independently associated with recurrence (HR 1.89, 95% CI 1.13–3.16) and death (HR 2.44, 1.39–4.28). Urbanisation showed no association; stage IV subgroups were too small to analyse. Limits: small exposed group, residence as exposure proxy, residual confounding.

Take-homeThese are the first data linking environmental exposure to ACC outcome. The finding needs confirmation, but it is directly relevant to Italian patients.

Radiology, pathology and diagnosis

Investigating the clinical utility of plasma succinate with insights from an Sdhb-deficient murine model

Cole YC et al. · Endocr Relat Cancer Full text read Hypothesis-generating

Prospective single-centre cohort (Cambridge, 2020–2024) of 122 people with PPGL or a germline PPGL predisposition, half of them SDHx carriers (185 plasma samples). In people without a tumour, plasma succinate separated SDHx carriers from non-carriers with an area under the ROC curve of 0.825 (cut-off 3.69 μM; sensitivity 75%, specificity 79.5%). In patients with a tumour, a cut-off of 2.76 μM separated SDH-deficient from SDH-proficient tumours (AUC 0.933; sensitivity 93.1%, specificity 86.4%). Succinate correlated with tumour size in a small subset (n=8) and did not differ between metastatic and non-metastatic SDH-deficient tumours. In Sdhb-deficient mice, succinate rose in adrenal tissue but not in plasma. Limits: single centre, cut-offs derived and tested in the same cohort, small subgroups.

Take-homePlasma succinate is a promising blood marker of SDHx status. The cut-offs need validation in an independent cohort before clinical use.

Genetics, surveillance and prognosis

Differences in management and outcomes between pheochromocytomas and sympathetic paragangliomas

Hermansson Salmi M et al. · Front Endocrinol Full text read Practice-informing

Retrospective cohort of 220 operated patients at Karolinska (2005–2025): 181 pheochromocytomas and 39 sympathetic PGL; 70.5% had germline panel testing (positive in 24.0% vs 32.4%, SDHB the main finding in PGL). PGL were more often operated open (73% vs 24%), laparoscopy was converted more often (30% vs 5.9%) and hospital stay was longer (7 vs 4 days). Recurrence, metastases or new metachronous tumours occurred in 27.8% vs 11.0% (p=0.019), despite shorter follow-up in PGL (mean 8.0 vs 11.0 years); overall survival did not differ. Dysglycaemia was less frequent in PGL and improved less after surgery (2/6, 33.3% vs 84.2%). Limits: single centre, retrospective, only 39 PGL.

Take-homeSympathetic PGL warrants closer long-term follow-up than adrenal pheochromocytoma.

Tumor risks and surveillance outcomes in SDHA variant carriers

Ellis K et al. · Endocr Oncol Full text read Hypothesis-generating

Single-institution cohort of 106 SDHA carriers (Utah, 2015–2025). 15 had an SDHA-associated tumour (median age 36): 7 head-and-neck PGL (none aggressive), 3 extra-adrenal PGL (all malignant) and 5 GIST (4 malignant). Of 83 unaffected carriers recommended for surveillance, only 35 completed at least one imaging study (49 studies, mostly FDG PET/CT); no new tumours were found, 21% had incidental benign findings needing further work-up, and baseline metanephrines were normal in all 48 tested. Limits: convenience sample, limited screening exposure, losses to follow-up.

Take-homeThe data support lighter surveillance for unaffected carriers, but the screened group is small. Affected carriers still need close oncological follow-up.

Minigene-based characterization and classification of splice-associated variants in succinate dehydrogenase B

Köhler A et al. · NPJ Precis Oncol Full text read Hypothesis-generating

Functional minigene (SDHB exons 2–5) with targeted RNA sequencing of 48 variants prioritised by SpliceAI, plus two negative controls. 38% of variants spliced normally and 34% almost entirely aberrantly; RNA evidence could be graded for 88% of aberrant transcripts. Among 26 classifiable variants, 13 (50%) were reclassified: 12 VUS to likely benign and one likely pathogenic to VUS. Limits: in vitro system, tumour RNA validation in only 2 cases, and conservative evidence weighting so about half of variants reached no actionable class.

Take-homeMinigene RNA testing can resolve many SDHB splice VUS, so fewer carriers enter unnecessary surveillance.
